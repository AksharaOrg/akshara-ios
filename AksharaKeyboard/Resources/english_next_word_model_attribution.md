English next-word model

`english_next_word_model.tsv` is the English top-50,000 ordered word-bigram table
from synth-inc/predictive-bigrams. It is derived from the Leipzig Corpora Collection
and redistributed under CC BY with attribution to the Leipzig project.

Source: https://github.com/synth-inc/predictive-bigrams
Corpus: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/
