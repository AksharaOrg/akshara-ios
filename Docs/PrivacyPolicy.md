# Akshara for iOS — Privacy Policy

Updated: 27 September 2026

Akshara processes keyboard input on your device. It does not transmit keystrokes, suggestions, clipboard contents, or analytics. It has no accounts, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Keyboard preferences and Full Access

Basic typing works without Full Access or an internet connection. Full Access enables shared local preferences, haptics, and optional Clipboard History. You can revoke it in iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Akshara. Revoking access does not erase previously saved data.

## Predictions and learned words

Bundled dictionaries provide suggestions. Akshara also learns words and word pairs from typing and suggestion selections in eligible text fields. That personal data is stored locally and retained until cleared. In Keyboard Settings, **Clear Learned Words** deletes learned data for both Sinhala and English, including cached predictions and pending saves. New typing can learn new words afterwards. Reset Keyboard Settings resets preferences but does not delete learned words.

## Clipboard History

Clipboard History is optional and off by default. With it enabled and Full Access allowed, Akshara captures copied text while the keyboard is open. iOS may ask for permission to paste. Recent history retains up to 10 clips, with each clip limited to 10,000 characters; new clips replace older ones. Pinned clips remain until manually deleted. Delete individual clips or clear recent history in Keyboard Settings. Turning the feature off stops capture but does not erase existing clips.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps up to 24 local crash and performance reports supplied by Apple. These contain technical information such as app version, device information, and call stacks, not keyboard input. In About → Diagnostics, you can delete reports or explicitly export and share them. Akshara does not upload reports automatically. Apple's system-level diagnostic sharing is controlled separately in iOS Settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use [Akshara's GitHub repository](https://github.com/AksharaOrg/akshara-ios) or [Akshara's website](https://akshara.org.lk). Do not include private typing or clipboard contents in a public issue.
